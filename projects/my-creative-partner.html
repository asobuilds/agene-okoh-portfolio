🧩 The Concept

What if you could speak or type your thoughts and watch them materialize in 3D? My Creative Partner is a philosophical quest game where users prompt a system with text and voice to create whatever they can imagine. The system interprets their intent and renders a 3D, 4K, HD visualization of their ideas — a new form of creative expression and self-exploration.

💡 What Makes It Interesting

  • Text and voice interface for prompting the system
  • Real-time 3D visualization of user ideas
  • 4K, HD rendering quality
  • Philosophical quest structure — users explore their own thoughts through visuals
  • Collaborative project (built with a team)
  • AI interprets intent and generates 3D scenes

⚙️ Technical Stack

  • creative-partner-app: Frontend application with virtual environment setup
  • server: Backend server handling AI processing and scene generation
  • Text and voice prompt processing
  • 3D rendering pipeline for real-time visualization
  • Unified AGENT.md blueprint for collaborative development

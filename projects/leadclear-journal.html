🧩 The Brief

A client needed a professional landing page for her digital product — a 30-day leadership journal. The page needed to communicate the product's value, build trust with her audience (students, young professionals, entrepreneurs, managers), and drive purchases through her existing Selar store.

💡 What I Built

  • Conversion-focused landing page with clear value proposition and social proof
  • Product walkthrough section showing the journal's structure (Plan → Act → Reflect → Improve)
  • Target audience segmentation (students, professionals, entrepreneurs, managers)
  • FAQ section to handle objections
  • Direct integration with Selar for product purchase (₦3,500)
  • Lead capture wired into Systeme.io funnel for email follow-up
  • Deployed on Vercel with instant, global CDN

⚙️ Technical Details

  • Static HTML/CSS/JS — fast, lightweight, no build step
  • Payment integration with Selar (hosted checkout link)
  • Systeme.io integration for lead capture and funnel automation
  • Responsive design optimized for mobile and desktop
  • Vercel deployment with automatic HTTPS

🎯 Outcome

A live, conversion-ready landing page that the client can point her audience to. Payments flow through Selar, and leads are captured automatically into her Systeme.io funnel for follow-up. This is a real business asset — not a demo.

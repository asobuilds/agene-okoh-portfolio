🧩 The Problem

Job seekers waste hours scrolling through irrelevant listings. They also struggle to build a professional CV without expensive tools or design skills. The entire process — CV creation, job search, and application — is fragmented across multiple platforms.

💡 The Solution

DevCraft Career consolidates everything into one place: users fill in their details, pick a CV template (with or without photo), attach certificates, and the system builds a professional CV. Then it automatically scans the internet for matching jobs — private, government, and freelance — and sends the user a notification with the direct apply link.

  • CV builder with multiple templates and certificate attachment
  • Automatic job matching based on skills and experience
  • Instant alerts with direct application links
  • Covers private, government, and freelance opportunities
  • Completely free for users

⚙️ Technical Highlights

  • Frontend: React with a clean, mobile-friendly CV builder interface
  • Backend: Python for job scraping and matching algorithms
  • Job aggregation from multiple sources (private, government, freelance)
  • Notification system that delivers matched jobs with apply links
  • Deployed on Vercel with zero-config static hosting

📊 Results

✅ Live and in use. Users have reported building their CV in under 10 minutes and getting matched to relevant jobs within a week. One user (Frontend Developer) was matched to a remote role; another (Data Analyst) said the alerts "actually match my skills."
